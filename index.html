Postdoctoral Researcher at Concordia University, working at the intersection of software engineering, artificial intelligence, and software maintenance.

I am a researcher and software engineer with interests in artificial intelligence, software engineering, and security. My research focuses on the long-term quality and comprehensibility of software systems. I investigate program comprehension and refactoring as two deeply intertwined concerns: understanding code is a prerequisite to improving it. I study both empirically; through mapping studies on program comprehension, and controlled experiments on open-source and in-house software for refactoring, including the impact of AI tool integration; with the overarching goal of building evidence-based recommendations that can serve as standards in these domains.
